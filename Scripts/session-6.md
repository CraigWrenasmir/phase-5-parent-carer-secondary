### Slide 2

Belonging, capacity and joy give us three connected questions to return to. Can your young person be recognised, included and able to participate as themselves? What does the day cost, and what protects or restores energy? Where are the interests, connections and moments of authentic enjoyment?

When you try a change at home or school, look across all three. Getting through a task is only part of the picture. The effect on energy and belonging matters too. Your young person's own response will help you judge whether a change is useful.
